## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose a digital platform called FARM2FORK that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and supermarkets. Farmers can upload their products, set prices, and receive orders directly from buyers. Consumers can find nearby farmers, compare prices, and purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable rates. The platform includes online payments, location-based search, and delivery management to eliminate unnecessary intermediaries, increase farmers' income, and reduce consumer prices.
## Technical Approach


We will develop a web-based application using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Django, and MySQL. Farmers can register and upload crop details, quantities, and prices. Consumers can search for nearby farmers, compare prices, and place orders directly. Google Maps API will help locate farmers, while UPI integration will support digital payments. A centralized database will manage products, users, and orders. The system will provide a simple, secure, and transparent platform to connect farmers directly with consumers and reduce unnecessary intermediaries.

## Feasibility and Viability

The proposed FARM2FORK platform is technically and economically feasible using affordable technologies such as Python Django, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and MySQL. It can be developed as a web application with low initial investment. Farmers and consumers can easily access the platform using smartphones. Revenue can be generated through small transaction fees or delivery charges. The platform can be expanded to different villages, cities, and states, making it a scalable and sustainable solution. It benefits farmers through higher income and consumers through affordable agricultural products.

## Impact and Benefits


1. Increases farmers' income by eliminating unnecessary middlemen.
2. Reduces agricultural product prices for consumers.
3. Provides fresh products directly from farmers.
4. Improves transparency in pricing and transactions.
5. Reduces food wastage through better demand management.
6. Creates more market opportunities for small-scale farmers.
7. Saves time and transportation costs.
8. Promotes digital technology in agriculture.

**Overall Impact:** Creates a fair, transparent, and efficient agricultural marketplace that benefits both farmers and consumers.
